# Engineering Evolution

An evidence-led field guide to the ideas, constraints, and trade-offs behind modern software systems. This is a growing research repository, not a glossary or interview cram sheet.

## Start here

1. [Master index](docs/INDEX.md) — navigation, research method, and learning path.
2. [Historical timeline](docs/TIMELINE.md) — a first-pass chronology of enabling breakthroughs.
3. [Big Tech engineering blog directory](docs/BIG_TECH_BLOGS.md) — official publications with recommended entry points.
4. [Engineering journal](journal/README.md) — record your own judgment after each case study.

## How chapters will work

Each major innovation gets a standalone case study under `chapters/`, organized around context, problem, bottleneck, alternatives, breakthrough, architecture, trade-offs, adoption, engineering lessons, and primary sources. Claims about a specific company's internals must be traceable to that company's own publication, a paper by its engineers, or a documented incident report. Interpretation will be labeled as interpretation.

The index and timeline are intentionally an orientation layer. They are not a substitute for the deeper, source-by-source chapters that follow.
